No API keys, accounts, inference server, or microphone are used by this test.
Initial preparation stores about 95 MB in CacheStorage. No asset fetches are
performed by synthesize() after initialization. The separate optional system
voice comparison uses the browser's speechSynthesis API, not the Piper model.
